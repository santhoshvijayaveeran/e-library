Getting Started

How do I create an account?
Click "Sign Up" from the homepage, enter your name, email, and password, and you're ready to start borrowing books right away.
Is Sanza Library free to use?
Yes, browsing the catalog and borrowing digital books is completely free for registered members.

Borrowing Books

How many books can I borrow at once?
You can have up to 5 books checked out at a time. Return or finish a title to free up a slot for another.
How long can I keep a borrowed book?
Most titles are borrowed for 14 days. You can see the exact due date for each book under "My Library."
What happens if I don't return a book on time?
Your account will be temporarily restricted from borrowing new titles until the overdue book is returned.

Account & Privacy

Can I change my email or password?
Yes, head to your profile settings to update your email address or password at any time.
How is my personal information used?
We only use your information to run your account and improve recommendations. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Support

I found an issue with a book listing. Who do I tell?
Email us at support@sanzalibrary.com with the book title and a description of the issue, and we'll take a look.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Reach out anytime at support@sanzalibrary.com.
